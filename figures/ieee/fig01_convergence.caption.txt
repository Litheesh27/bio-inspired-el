curve is coloured and its panel title labelled by the PROVENANCE of its
reported path -- only max_flux_walk is the model's own extraction; the two
fallbacks are Dijkstra on a subgraph, so a green curve reaching 1.0 is not the
model solving the problem. ILLUSTRATIVE: regenerated by a seeded re-run (Phase
